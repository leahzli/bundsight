About

BundSight is run by Leah Zhang.

Leah is a Shanghai local, born here and based here, at home in Shanghainese as well as Mandarin and English. She trained as a lawyer, with an LL.M. from the University of Michigan and PRC qualification, and spent years on cross-border transactions before starting BundSight.

It means she reads a factory quotation, a distribution term sheet or a joint-venture proposal the way your counterparties do, and tells you what is actually on the table.

Questions

Before you write to us.

Do you arrange visas, flights or hotels?

No. BundSight is a business-visit service, not a travel agency. We are happy to recommend hotels close to your meetings.

Which languages do you work in?

English and Mandarin, and Shanghainese, which still opens doors in this city. Other languages on request, with notice.

Where do you operate?

Shanghai and the Yangtze River Delta: Suzhou, Kunshan, Hangzhou, Ningbo. Other cities by arrangement, with travel billed separately.

How do I book?

Send your dates and a rough agenda. You will have a written quote within 12 hours. A 30% deposit confirms the booking; the balance is due after the visit.

How do you take payment?

Bank transfer, Wise, PayPal or card.

What happens when the visit turns into a deal?

We stay on. Term summaries, follow-up with the counterparties, and a review of the paperwork before you commit to anything.
